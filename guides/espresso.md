# Espresso

**Dose:** 18 g in, 36 g out (1:2)
**Time:** 27 to 32 seconds
**Grind:** fine; adjust in small steps

1. Dose 18 g into a clean, dry basket and distribute evenly.
2. Tamp level with firm, even pressure.
3. Start the shot and stop at 36 g in the cup.
4. Taste. Fast and sour means grind finer; slow and bitter means coarser.

On the storefront, choose the **espresso** grind option when ordering pre-ground coffee for an espresso machine.

_Try it with: Holiday Blend as a bright single-origin-style shot, or house espresso for milk drinks._
