# French press

**Ratio:** 1:15 (60 g coffee to 900 g water)
**Grind:** coarse, like sea salt
**Water:** 94 °C

1. Add coffee to the press and pour all the water.
2. Stir gently after 1 minute.
3. At 4:00, skim the crust off the top with two spoons.
4. Put the lid on, press slowly, and pour right away so it doesn't keep extracting.

_Try it with: our house espresso roast for a heavy, chocolatey cup._
