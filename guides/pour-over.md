# Pour-over

**Ratio:** 1:16 (22 g coffee to 350 g water)
**Grind:** medium-fine, like table salt
**Water:** 94 °C (just off the boil)

1. Rinse the paper filter with hot water and discard the rinse water.
2. Add 22 g of coffee and level the bed.
3. Bloom: pour 50 g of water, wait 40 seconds.
4. Pour slowly in circles to 200 g by 1:15, then to 350 g by 2:00.
5. Let it drain. Total time should be about 3:00 to 3:30.

**Too sour?** Grind finer. **Too bitter?** Grind coarser.

_Try it with: Holiday Blend. The Yirgacheffe berry notes shine on pour-over._
